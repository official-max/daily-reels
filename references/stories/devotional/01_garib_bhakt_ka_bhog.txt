Garib bhakt ka bhog (channel owner ke storyboard se)

Ek gaon mein ek garib ladka rehta tha. Phata kurta, kandhe par jhola, par chehre par hamesha muskaan.
Kai din aise aate jab uski thaali khaali rehti. Wo udaas baith jaata, gaal par haath rakhkar sochta... phir bhi shikayat nahi karta.

Har shaam wo apni chhoti si kothri mein Krishna ji ki murti ke aage diya jalata aur aankhein band karke prarthna karta:
"Prabhu, mere paas dene ko kuch nahi, bas mera pyaar hai."

Ek raat, chaand ki roshni mein, wo ped ke neeche baitha. Jo thode se phal mile the, wo khud nahi khaaye...
thaali mein sajakar Krishna ji ko bhog lagane ke liye utha liye.

Tabhi chaaron taraf sunehri roshni phail gayi. Shri Krishna khud prakat hue, muskuraate hue haath badhaaya.
Ladka hairaan reh gaya. Krishna ji uske paas baith gaye, jaise purane dost hon, aur pyaar se baatein karne lage.
Ladke ne kaanpte haathon se wahi phal Krishna ji ko arpit kiye. Krishna ji ne bade prem se swikaar kiye
aur bole: "Tumne jo diya, wo sone se bhi keemti hai, kyunki usme sachcha prem hai."

Phir unhone ladke ke sir par haath rakhkar aashirwad diya. Unke haath ke ishaare se ek sundar ghar ban gaya.
Ladka khushi se baahein phailaakar nayi zindagi ka swagat karta hai.

Par sabse sundar baat: ghar milne ke baad bhi wo roz shaam diya jalakar usi prem se Krishna ji ki prarthna karta raha.
Aakhir mein dono dost suraj dhalte hue pahaadi par saath baithe hain.

Seekh: Bhagwan ko daulat nahi, sachcha prem chahiye. Aur jab sab kuch mil jaaye, tab bhi shukriya karna mat bhoolo.
